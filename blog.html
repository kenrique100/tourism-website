Lela Cultural Festival

September 2025 by Admin

The Lela Cultural Festival, celebrated by the people of the Bali Nyonga in Cameroon, is a vibrant display of tradition, music, and unity. It marks a time of thanksgiving, ancestral homage, and cultural pride, bringing together locals and visitors in a rich celebration of heritage.

Tour to the Abi Resort

September 2025 by Admin

Nestled in the heart of Cameroon's lush South West region, Abi Resort offers a peaceful escape with its natural charm, scenic landscapes, and luxurious amenities. From guided nature walks to lakeside relaxation, this tour promises serenity and unforgettable experiences.

Bimbia Historical Slave Port

September 2025 by Admin

The Bimbia Slave Port, located along the coast of Limbe, stands as a solemn reminder of Cameroon’s role in the transatlantic slave trade. Visitors to this historic site walk through the very paths where thousands were once shipped into slavery, making it a powerful destination for reflection and remembrance.
